PRIVACY POLICY

Your scores stay with you.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

No personal data collected

NETS does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account or a sign-in.

No tracking or advertising

NETS does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, location services, or third-party advertising networks.

Shot counts

Your IN and OUT counts are used only to display the current session in the app. Tapping RESET clears those counts.

Optional voice mode

Voice mode is off unless you turn it on. When enabled, NETS asks iPhone for microphone and speech-recognition permission to understand the counting commands. NETS does not record, retain, or share audio or speech transcripts. Speech recognition is provided by Apple system services and may depend on your device or Internet connection.

Children's privacy

Because NETS does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
