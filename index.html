NETBALL SHOT COUNTER

Keep your focus on the court.

NETS is a simple, fast counter for recording successful shots and outs during netball practice, with optional voice control when your hands are busy.

HOW IT WORKS

Count. Review. Reset.

  1. 01

    Record each shot

    Tap IN for a successful shot or OUT for a shot that does not go in.

  2. 02

    Check your result

    Tap DONE to reveal the NETS and OUTS totals for that run.

  3. 03

    See your accuracy

    Tap PERCENTAGE to view IN as a percentage of all recorded shots.

  4. 04

    Start again

    Tap RESET to return both counts to zero for a fresh session.

VOICE CONTROL

Count without touching the screen.

  1. 01

    Turn voice on

    Tap VOICE OFF at the top of the screen. It changes to VOICE ON while NETS is listening. The first time, allow Microphone and Speech Recognition access when iPhone asks.

  2. 02

    Use the counting commands

    Say “COUNT IN” for a successful shot, “COUNT OUT” for a shot that does not go in, or “RESET COUNT” to begin again from zero.

  3. 03

    Ask for your percentage

    Say “PERCENTAGE” to show and hear your current IN percentage.

  4. 04

    Listen for confirmation

    Keep the speaker icon on to hear the clear confirmation tones and spoken percentage. Voice mode is designed for a quiet practice area; tap VOICE ON to turn listening off.

QUICK TIPS

Built for the side of the court.

Use the speaker icon to switch tap sounds on or off. Your counts are kept on the device for the current session and are cleared when you tap RESET.
